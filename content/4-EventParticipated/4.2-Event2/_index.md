#### Report: “FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!”

### Event Objectives

- Share the journey and experiences of high-achieving teams in the Agentic AI Build Week (AABW) competition.
- Introduce ideas for applying Agentic AI to real-world business problems.
- Learn how teams build AI Agent solutions on the AWS platform.
- Learn innovative thinking and adaptability to the rapid development of AI technology.

### Speakers

- **Giuseppe Marazzotta** - Head of Technology, AWS
- Teams that participated in AABW.

### Key Highlights

#### Sharing from Giuseppe Marazzotta (Head of Technology, AWS)

#### The Change in Technology Development Speed

Speaker Giuseppe Marazzotta shared about the rapid changes in technology development speed across different periods.

- In the past, releasing a software update could take several weeks or even months to complete.
- Today, with the emergence of Agentic AI, AI Agents are capable of supporting automation in many stages of the development process, from requirement analysis, source code generation, to deploying changes much faster.
- This creates a new era where the ability to adapt and learn quickly becomes an important advantage for technology engineers.

#### Innovation Mindset (Mental Model)

One of the important messages shared was that young engineers need to proactively change their way of thinking and not be limited by traditional approaches.

- The younger generation has a major advantage because they are not constrained by legacy systems or mindsets that have existed for decades.
- Continuously learning, experimenting with new technologies, and integrating AI into workflows will help young builders quickly become future leaders.
- AI is not only a supporting tool but also a factor that expands creativity and problem-solving capabilities.

#### AI and Large-Scale Automation

The speaker also shared practical examples of applying automation in large-scale systems.

- Amazon currently operates more than one million robots in distribution centers worldwide.
- However, although automation technology continues to develop strongly, humans still play an important role in designing, managing, and directing these systems.
- Technology only creates real value when combined with human problem-solving thinking.

### Outstanding Projects from AABW Competition

### Team Top 1 (Champion)

#### Idea and Solution

The winning team developed a **Multi-Agent System** to optimize workflows and support decision-making in enterprise environments.

#### Technical Architecture

The solution uses multiple AI Agents with specialized roles:

- **Planner Agent:** Creates plans and divides tasks.
- **Execution Agent:** Performs assigned tasks.
- **Reviewer Agent:** Checks and evaluates output results.

The system was built using technologies such as **AWS Bedrock** and **LangChain**, while integrating **Guardrails** to:

- Control the accuracy of results.
- Reduce AI-generated incorrect information (hallucination).
- Ensure data safety and security.

#### Highlights

- Includes a complete demo with automatic error handling through a **self-healing loop** mechanism.
- The solution addresses real business problems by reducing operational costs and increasing work processing speed.

---

### Team Top 2 (Runner-up)

#### Idea and Solution

The team developed a platform that automates data analysis and generates intelligent reports using Agentic AI.

#### Architecture and Features

The system is capable of:

- Connecting to raw data sources.
- Using AI Agents to query, process, and analyze data.
- Automatically extracting important information without requiring many manual operations from Data Analysts.

#### Evaluation from Judges

- The idea has high practicality and good scalability.
- The team demonstrated mature thinking by recognizing that a product developed within 48 hours is only a Proof of Concept (POC).
- Team members also clearly analyzed challenges when deploying the system into a Production environment, such as risk control, operational costs, and scalability.

---

### SA App Project (Solution Architect / System Assistant App)

#### Idea and Solution

SA App is an AI Agent assistant that supports Solution Architects during the process of designing, reviewing, and optimizing AWS system architectures.

#### Main Features

- **Automatic Architecture Evaluation:** Compares system designs with standards from the AWS Well-Architected Framework.
- **Cost Optimization:** Provides suggestions to reduce cloud resource costs.
- **Security Enhancement:** Detects security issues in system configurations.

#### Evaluation

The solution has high practical value for Cloud engineers, significantly reducing manual review time and improving the efficiency of architecture design processes.

---

### SHEPHERD Project

#### Idea and Objective

SHEPHERD is an AI Agent solution that acts as a "shepherd", responsible for coordinating and monitoring a system consisting of multiple AI Agents.

#### Operation

The system supports:

- Managing task lifecycles.
- Coordinating workflows between Agents.
- Checking the output quality of each Agent.
- Ensuring Agents operate according to objectives and comply with defined regulations.

#### Highlights

The project lays the foundation for managing Agent-to-Agent (A2A) systems, which is an important development direction as AI systems become increasingly complex.

---

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML Agent) Project

#### Project Idea and Problem Statement

The project applies **Agentic AI** in the financial and banking sector to detect and prevent fraudulent activities and money laundering in real time.

#### Processing Approach

The AI Agent is capable of:

- Analyzing transaction behaviors and detecting abnormal patterns.
- Linking data across multiple accounts and different time periods.
- Automatically compiling suspicious activity profiles.
- Checking watchlists and generating reports to support Risk and Compliance teams.

#### Value Delivered

- Reducing the false-positive rate of alerts.
- Shortening the investigation time for suspicious transactions.
- Helping risk control staff process and analyze information more efficiently.
